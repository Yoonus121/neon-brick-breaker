BRICK BREAKER
0000 Level 01

HOW TO PLAY

  • Press SPACE to start the game.
  • Aim and launch the ball towards the bricks.
  • Break bricks to earn points and increase your score.
  • Keep the ball from falling below the paddle.
  • You have 3 lives. You lose a life whenever you fail to keep the ball in play.
  • Break all the bricks to complete the level.
